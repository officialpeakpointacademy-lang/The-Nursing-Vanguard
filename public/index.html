WHO WE ARE

Building a stronger community of future nurses.

The Nursing Vanguard is a private learning and collaboration platform created to bring nursing students together beyond the traditional classroom.

We provide a shared space where members can access academic resources, document their learning, exchange ideas, participate in challenges and support one another throughout their academic journey.

Our goal is simple: to encourage consistency, strengthen academic collaboration and build a community where everyone has room to grow.
